Case Study 01

Star Palace Rameshwaram

Rameshwaram, Tamil Nadu · Pilgrim & leisure hotel

~15%YoY revenue growth

The challenge

The property had quality infrastructure and strong guest service standards, but limited online visibility and no meaningful presence across major OTA platforms restricted market reach and booking potential. Search visibility and OTA ranking were also weak.

The approach

OTA integration and distribution expansion were combined with optimized listings, keyword-focused descriptions, accurate room details, pricing and inventory management, visibility optimization and structured reputation management.

Case Study 02

Hotel Spencer, Kharghar

Kharghar, Navi Mumbai · Business-budget luxury segment

₹3.8–4.8KARR sustained

The challenge

The hotel needed more consistent occupancy and optimized revenue performance throughout the week. Key issues included weekday dips, limited targeting of corporate and transit guests, and OTA listings that required further optimization.

The approach

A focused revenue-management strategy introduced dynamic pricing based on demand patterns, smart pricing for low-demand weekdays and targeted offers for short stays and corporate guests while maintaining rate integrity.

Revenue ManagementDynamic PricingOTA OptimizationDemand Segmentation
₹3,800–₹4,800ARR range sustained
↑OTA conversion performance
↑Inventory utilization efficiency
YoYARR & room-night growth

The strategy improved revenue quality, pricing consistency and occupancy balance. The case study also identifies further opportunity through refined weekday pricing and stronger demand-based rate control.

Case Study 03

Ora Lake View Hotel

Niederried bei Interlaken, Switzerland · Lakeside leisure hotel

37%room revenue growth

The challenge

The hotel relied on static pricing, had lower-than-potential Booking.com visibility and carried a 7.5 guest rating. Its strong lakeside setting also presented an untapped wellness opportunity.

The approach

Demand-based dynamic pricing used market demand, booking pace, competitor analysis, seasonal trends and inventory availability. OTA content, imagery, availability, pricing and conversion-focused promotions were also optimized, alongside structured review management.

Within 90 days, the property achieved 37% revenue growth, 280 additional room nights, a 10-point occupancy increase, #1 Booking.com ranking and improved guest ratings.
