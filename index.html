Clear answers before you sign

Will RevAlpha guarantee my revenue?

No. Revenue depends on demand, product quality, reputation, operations, inventory and market conditions. We provide professional revenue strategy, execution and monitoring — not unrealistic guarantees.

Do you manage OTAs?

Yes, within the agreed scope. But RevAlpha is not just OTA management. OTAs are one part of your wider pricing and distribution strategy.

Do I need an in-house Revenue Manager too?

For many independent hotels, no — our plans are designed to work like an outsourced revenue department. Larger hotels may use RevAlpha alongside an internal team.

Can you work with my PMS or channel manager?

Usually yes, subject to access and your technology setup. Third-party software fees remain separate unless specifically included.

Do you also provide marketing?

RevAlpha stays focused on revenue. Social media, SEO, websites and paid campaigns can be supported through Hospitality Minds when required.

How quickly can we start?

After commercial confirmation, we begin with access collection, baseline analysis, competitor validation and a structured onboarding plan.
