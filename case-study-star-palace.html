Star Palace Rameshwaram

Transforming digital visibility and OTA performance in a pilgrim and leisure destination.

Rameshwaram, Tamil NaduPilgrim & Leisure Hotel
~15%Approximate year-on-year revenue growth

Background

Star Palace Rameshwaram is a pilgrim and leisure hotel in Rameshwaram, Tamil Nadu. The property primarily serves religious travelers visiting Ramanathaswamy Temple, along with families and leisure guests.

Although the hotel had quality infrastructure and strong guest service standards, limited online visibility and absence from major OTA platforms restricted market reach and booking potential.

Objective

The objective was to establish a stronger digital presence and create a more consistent OTA-driven demand engine.

  • Create visibility across major OTA platforms.
  • Improve search visibility for relevant Rameshwaram hotel queries.
  • Increase online bookings and occupancy.
  • Strengthen guest reputation and review management.
  • Build long-term growth through digital distribution.

Services deployed

OTA Management
Revenue Management
Online Reputation Management
Digital Visibility Optimization

The transformation

The strategy focused on distribution, visibility, conversion and reputation, creating a stronger digital demand engine for the property.

Distribution expansion

The property was onboarded across major OTA platforms with optimized listings, room content, pricing and inventory management.

Visibility optimization

Keyword optimization, competitive pricing, enhanced room content, inventory synchronization and review monitoring were used to improve digital visibility and conversion opportunities.

Reputation management

Review collection, guest feedback management and structured responses supported a stronger reputation across OTA channels.

Measured commercial outcomes

~15%Approx. YoY revenue growth
#1TripAdvisor ranking in destination
↑Online bookings & occupancy
↑OTA visibility & market reach

Current position

The property continues to generate substantial bookings through OTA channels and benefits from stronger digital presence, positive guest reviews and stable occupancy performance. The story demonstrates how a destination hotel can combine digital distribution, revenue management and reputation work into a more consistent commercial process.
