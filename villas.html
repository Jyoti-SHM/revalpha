Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get my villa or homestay live on OTAs?

Most listings go live within 10–15 business days, once we have your property details, photos and required documents — individual OTA verification times can vary.

What documents are needed to list my property?

PAN card, Aadhar card, photos, cancelled cheque.

Who manages my rates and OTAs after the setup is done?

After one-time setup of rates and offers, training will be provided to your staff to manage the OTAs.

Does this plan cover ongoing OTA management?

No, Express Setup is a one-time service covering listing, rates, offers and staff training. Ongoing day-to-day management can be taken up separately if you need it.

Is there any renewal or lock-in for this plan?

No. Express Setup is a one-time payment with no renewal or lock-in — it's a single setup engagement.
