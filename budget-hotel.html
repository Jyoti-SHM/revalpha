Frequently asked questions

How much time will it take to get live on OTAs?

International OTAs will approximately be live in 2–3 business days, while domestic OTAs can take up to 2 weeks to go live, depending on documents and the past history of listings.

What documents are needed to get live on OTAs?

GST, PAN card, a cancelled cheque, Aadhar card, latest electricity bill, tourism certificate (for certain states), and lease agreement.

Do I need to hire my own revenue manager?

No. Our team acts as your revenue management function, so you get a trained team working on your pricing without the cost of hiring one in-house.

Will I lose control over my pricing decisions?

No. Rates and offers are set within limits you approve, and you can review or override any pricing decision at any time.

Is there a lock-in period?

Express Setup is a one-time engagement. The AMC and Standard Revenue Management Plan run for the period agreed at signup, and either side can choose not to renew at the end of that term.
